# Mutual Action Plan (MAP)

**Purpose:** A shared, dated plan that both buyer and seller agree to, working backward from the buyer's target decision date. It turns "we are interested" into a sequence of commitments, and it exposes problems early.

**When to propose one:** After discovery has produced a confirmed pain, a named business owner, and an outline of the decision process (scorecard total roughly 11 or higher; see `01-meddpicc-scorecard.md`).

**How to introduce it (suggested wording):**
> "To make sure we reach a decision on your timeline and don't hit surprises with legal, security, or procurement, I'd like to build a shared plan with you. Can we work backward from the date you need this live?"

---

## Rules

1. **Start from the buyer's date**, not yours, and work backward.
2. **Every step has an owner and a date.** Owners are on both sides.
3. **Include the unglamorous gates:** InfoSec review, legal and MSA redlines, procurement onboarding, budget approval, and signature.
4. **The buyer edits it.** If the buyer has not changed anything, they probably have not read it.
5. **Review it on every call.** Missed dates are information; ask why.
6. **Do not send it as a PDF.** Use something the buyer can edit and comment on.

## Reading the plan: warning signs

| Signal | What it usually means | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer will not name owners or dates | Low commitment or no real timeline | Return to Metrics, Pain, and Champion in the scorecard |
| Economic Buyer step keeps slipping | Champion lacks access or the deal is not a priority | Ask the Champion directly what is blocking access |
| Security or legal not started inside 60 days of target | Late paper; close date at risk | Start in parallel now, before the proposal is final |
| Dates move twice with no reason | Deal is stalling | Ask "what has changed?" and rescore |
| Buyer adds a new stakeholder late | Buying committee grew | Update the stakeholder map and re-run discovery with the new person |
