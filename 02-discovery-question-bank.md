# Discovery Question Bank by Persona

**How to use:** Pick 6-8 questions for a call. Do not run the list. Each question is paired with what a *useful* answer sounds like, so you can tell when to push for more.

**Principle:** The goal of discovery is to leave with evidence for the scorecard (`01-meddpicc-scorecard.md`) and a clearer picture of the buyer's problem than they had when the call started. If they learn something about their own situation, you are doing it well.

---

## Universal questions (any persona)

| Question | Listen for |
|---|---|
| What prompted you to take this meeting now? | A trigger event (new leader, board request, budget cycle, failed initiative) |
| What happens if nothing changes in the next 12 months? | A consequence with a cost, owner, or deadline |
| How are you handling this today, and what is wrong with that approach? | Specific tools, people, or spreadsheets, plus a real frustration |
| Who else is affected by this, and who would need to agree to change it? | Names and roles, the start of the buying committee |
| How would you know in six months that this worked? | A metric, a baseline, a target (feeds Metrics) |
| Have you looked at other approaches or vendors? | Alternatives, including internal builds and doing nothing |
| What would need to be true for this to be a priority this quarter? | Budget, sponsor, timing constraints |

## HR / People Analytics / Workforce Planning leaders

| Question | Listen for |
|---|---|
| When leadership asks a workforce question you cannot answer quickly, what is it usually about? | The recurring unanswered question, which is the product requirement |
| How long does it take to get from a question to a defensible answer? | Days or weeks of manual work; dependence on consultants or analysts |
| Where does external market data enter your planning today, if at all? | Gaps, stale data, annual cycles |
| How do you present workforce recommendations to Finance? | Whether the analysis connects to cost and business drivers |
| Which decisions were made in the last year on data you did not fully trust? | Credibility gaps and appetite for validation |

## Talent Acquisition leaders

| Question | Listen for |
|---|---|
| Which roles are hardest to fill, and how do you know? | Evidence versus anecdote |
| How do you decide where to hire and what to offer? | Whether location, competitor, and compensation decisions use data |
| What do hiring managers push back on most? | Credibility of recommendations; a use case for defensible data |
| Which metrics do you report that you do not fully trust? | Data quality issues |
| What is a long-open requisition costing the business? | Whether they can quantify vacancy cost |

## CMOs / Marketing, Brand, and Insights leaders

| Question | Listen for |
|---|---|
| What decisions are you being asked to defend to the CEO or CFO? | Pressure that makes research or data matter |
| Which of your current research or data sources actually changes a decision? | Shelfware versus used tools |
| How does insight travel from your team to people who make campaign or product decisions? | Adoption and usage gaps |
| What would you do with more speed, or a segment you cannot see today? | Unmet need |
| How do you currently prove the return on research or data spend? | Renewal risk and ROI language |

## CFOs and Finance leaders

| Question | Listen for |
|---|---|
| What is the biggest forecast or planning uncertainty you carry this year? | Where external data could reduce risk |
| How do you evaluate a new vendor investment? | Hurdle rates, payback expectations, approval thresholds |
| Where do you rely on outside advisors for work you would rather own? | Consulting spend as a budget source |
| Who else needs to be involved before you commit to a multi-year agreement? | Procurement, legal, and risk requirements |
| What would make a multi-year term attractive or unattractive? | Terms, flexibility, price protection |

## IT, InfoSec, and Procurement

| Question | Listen for |
|---|---|
| What does your review process require, and how long does it usually take? | Real timeline, documents, and reviewers (feeds Paper Process) |
| What has stalled or killed vendor reviews in the past? | Common blockers to address early |
| How does AI or data from external vendors fit your policies? | Governance concerns about data use and AI features |
| Which integrations or data flows matter most? | Technical requirements |
| Who signs, and at what threshold? | Signature authority |

## Questions that test your own assumptions

| Question | Why ask it |
|---|---|
| "What am I missing about how you would evaluate this?" | Surfaces hidden criteria |
| "Who would disagree with this plan, and why?" | Finds the blocker |
| "What would make you say no?" | Finds the disqualifier |
