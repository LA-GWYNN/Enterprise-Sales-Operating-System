# Enterprise-Sales-Operating-System
A working set of templates for running a complex B2B sales cycle: qualifying, discovering, planning, inspecting deals, and ramping in a new territory.

These are **templates and frameworks**, not case studies. They contain no employer data, client names, pricing, or deal details. Any numbers shown are **illustrative placeholders** and are labeled as such.

## What's inside

| File | Use it when | What it forces you to do |
|---|---|---|
| `01-meddpicc-scorecard.md` | Qualifying or forecasting a deal | Score each element on evidence, not feeling, and apply a decision rule |
| `02-discovery-question-bank.md` | Preparing for a first or second call | Match questions to the persona and listen for specific answers |
| `03-mutual-action-plan.md` | A deal is real and heading to a decision | Agree on dated steps with the buyer, including legal, procurement and InfoSec |
| `04-deal-risk-review.md` | Weekly pipeline or deal inspection | Separate facts from hopes and assign a next action to every risk |
| `05-30-60-90-template.md` | Starting in a new role or territory | State assumptions, targets, and what would change the plan |

## Design principles

1. **Evidence over optimism.** Every score or stage needs something you could show a skeptical sales leader.
2. **Exit criteria, not stage names.** A deal advances because something happened on the buyer's side, not because time passed.
3. **Every risk gets an owner and a date.** A risk with no next action is just a worry.
4. **Assumptions are labeled.** Fact, assumption, hypothesis, and estimate are kept apart.
5. **Calibrate against your own data.** Thresholds in these templates are starting hypotheses. Adjust them against your win/loss history.

## How the pieces connect

Discovery (02) produces the evidence that fills the scorecard (01). A scorecard above your threshold earns a mutual action plan (03). The plan's milestones and the scorecard's gaps feed the weekly inspection (04). The 30/60/90 (05) sets up this system in a new territory.
