# MEDDPICC Qualification Scorecard

**Purpose:** Turn qualification into an evidence test. Use it at stage gates and before forecasting a deal above "pipeline."

**Scoring (per element):**
- **0 = Unknown.** No evidence, or only your assumption.
- **1 = Partial.** Some evidence, but from one source, secondhand, or not yet validated by the buyer.
- **2 = Confirmed.** Evidence from the buyer in their own words or documents, ideally from more than one person.

## The scorecard

| Element | The question you must be able to answer | Evidence required for a 2 | Common red flags | Score | Evidence / source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M**etrics | What measurable outcome does the buyer expect, and what is it worth to them? | Buyer states the metric, baseline, and target, and agrees on how it would be measured | Value described only in general terms ("efficiency", "insights") | | |
| **E**conomic Buyer | Who owns the budget and can say yes without anyone else's approval? | You have met them, or have a documented path to them, and know their priorities | Champion says "I'll take it upstairs" and you have no access | | |
| **D**ecision Criteria | What will the buyer use to choose, and who set the criteria? | Criteria are written down, you know the weighting, and you have shaped at least one | Criteria were written by a competitor or by someone you have never met | | |
| **D**ecision Process | What steps, approvals, and reviews happen between now and signature? | Steps are mapped with owners and dates, including legal, procurement, InfoSec and finance | "We'll figure out the process when we get there" | | |
| **P**aper Process | What is the contracting path (MSA, security review, procurement, signature authority)? | Documents requested, reviewers named, typical timeline confirmed by someone who has done it | Security or legal not yet engaged with a close date inside 60 days | | |
| **I**dentified Pain | What problem is costly enough that they will act this quarter? | Buyer describes the pain and its consequences; it is tied to a business owner | Pain is yours, not theirs; "nice to have" | | |
| **C**hampion | Who has power, influence, and a personal win, and will sell when you are not there? | Champion has coached you, shared internal information, and taken a visible action on your behalf | A friendly contact with no influence or no willingness to act | | |
| **C**ompetition | What is the buyer's alternative, including doing nothing, an internal build, or an incumbent? | You know the alternative, why it is attractive, and how you differ on the buyer's criteria | "No competition" (usually means status quo or an unknown) | | |

## Decision rules (starting hypotheses: calibrate to your own win/loss data)

| Total | Rule |
|---|---|
| 0-6 | Do not forecast. Run discovery to fill gaps or disqualify. |
| 7-10 | Pipeline only. Name the three lowest elements and a next action for each. |
| 11-13 | Credible. Build the mutual action plan and plan access to the Economic Buyer. |
| 14-16 | Forecastable. Inspect Paper Process and Competition for late surprises. |

**Override rules (apply regardless of total):**
- Economic Buyer = 0 **and** Paper Process = 0: cap the deal at pipeline.
- Champion = 0: you have a contact, not a champion. Do not advance on enthusiasm alone.
- Identified Pain = 0 or 1: the most likely outcome is "no decision." Re-qualify before investing more.

## Stage exit criteria (example structure, adapt to your process)

| Stage | Exit requires |
|---|---|
| Qualify | Pain confirmed by buyer, a named business owner, and a reason to act now |
| Discover | Metrics drafted, decision process outlined, Champion identified |
| Validate / Demo | Buyer has seen the product against their use case; criteria confirmed; Economic Buyer access planned |
| Propose | Business case agreed in principle; paper process started in parallel |
| Negotiate / Close | Mutual action plan on track; legal, procurement, and security reviews in progress with dates |

## Failure modes and corrections

| Failure mode | What it looks like | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Happy ears | Score rises after a good call with no new buyer evidence | Rescore only when a document, a name, or a date is added |
| Single-threaded | All evidence comes from one contact | Require a second source for any 2 |
| Late paper | Security or legal first appears at proposal | Ask about the paper process during discovery |
| Hidden competitor | "No competition" | Ask what they would do if they did not buy |
| Sandbagged gaps | Zeros are left blank | Write "0 - unknown" and a next action |
